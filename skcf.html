Who we are

Giving children a fair start

Based in Delhi, we work with underprivileged children and their families across India. Through academic classes, workshops and distribution drives, we make sure a child's background never limits their future.

Our belief: education, a healthy meal and a warm set of clothes are the foundation of every childhood.

“A child who is fed, clothed and taught can change the story of an entire family.”— SK Children Foundation

Our work

How we support children

Our programmes run throughout the year to support learning, health and wellbeing.

🏫

Academic Classes

Free, regular classes for children who cannot access quality schooling.

  • Reading, writing and maths foundations
  • Homework and exam support
🛠️

Workshops

Interactive sessions that build skills and confidence.

  • Art, craft and creativity
  • Life skills, hygiene and digital awareness
🍱

Nutrition Drives

Meals and food distribution to fight hunger and support healthy growth.

  • Regular meal programmes
  • Festive-season food packs
🧥

Clothing Distribution

Clothing drives that keep children comfortable year-round.

  • Winter wear drives
  • School and festival outfits
🌟

Holistic Development

Activities that support the whole child, not only academics.

  • Sports and games
  • Mentoring and storytelling
🤝

Community Outreach

Reaching families and neighbourhoods across India through volunteers and partners.

  • Awareness campaigns
  • Corporate & student volunteering
